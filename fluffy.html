Zero prerecorded videos, zero static sprites, and zero external 3D model dependencies—every strand of fur, shadow, and sound wave is computed in real time.

38,000 Bézier Silk Hair Filaments

Hardware-instanced 10-segment quadratic Bézier ribbons with Kajiya-Kay / Marschner dual-lobe anisotropic sheen, root-to-tip self-occlusion, and a 3D facial repulsion shield that keeps the eyes 100% clear.

Use the animated 3D Salon Scissors to trim hair locally per physics cluster, blast directional air waves with the 3D Blow-Dryer, apply Rainbow/Ombré tip dye, or drink the Magic Grow Potion.

Bath Mode & Wet-Fur Gravity Physics

Lather Fluffy with clinging 3D soap bubbles, rinse with a water shower that dynamically weighs down and sleekens the coat, then shake or blow-dry back to full cloud volume.

Toss a bouncing 3D Yarn Ball or shine a 3D Laser Pointer for Fluffy to chase, feed a 3D Berry Macaron with chewing animations, or start a Web Audio chiptune Dance Party.

Download Fluffy for Offline Mobile & Desktop Play

FLUFFY is packaged as a zero-dependency, self-contained HTML5 + WebGL2 application with an embedded offline Three.js fallback engine and PWA Service Worker. Download the standalone file once and play offline anywhere—on phones, tablets, or laptops with zero internet connection required.

How to Install on Mobile (Offline Mode)

  • Step 1 (Download): Tap "Download Offline App" above to save fluffy_offline.html directly to your phone or computer.
  • Step 2 (Android / Chrome): Open the downloaded file in Chrome or tap Menu (⋮) → "Add to Home Screen" / "Install App" for a full-screen offline icon.
  • Step 3 (iOS / Safari): Open in Safari or the Files app, tap Share → "Add to Home Screen" to launch Fluffy like a native mobile game anytime offline.

              PRIVACY POLICY (EFFECTIVE 2026)
              
100% Client-Side Privacy Guarantee

              
Zero Personal Data Collection: FLUFFY runs entirely inside your local web browser using WebGL2 and the Web Audio API. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, IP addresses, or usage analytics.

              
Local Device Storage Only: Your pet's name, fur color, accessory choices, and happiness stats are saved strictly in your browser's local window.localStorage under key fluffy_ultimate_save_v12 and can be wiped at any time via Settings → Reset Saved Settings.

            FAQ & TECHNICAL Q&A
            
Frequently Asked Questions

          

          

            

              How does FLUFFY render 38,000 3D hair strands at 60 FPS in a web browser?
              
FLUFFY uses WebGL2 hardware instancing (gl.drawElementsInstanced) to render 38,000 10-segment quadratic Bézier silk ribbons in a single GPU draw call, driven by 64 Verlet/spring physics guide clusters across 5 articulated body parts (torso, head, left ear, right ear, and tail).

            

            

              Can I download FLUFFY and play offline on my mobile phone or desktop?
              
Yes! Click the "Download Offline App (fluffy_offline.html)" button on this website to save the complete self-contained HTML5 file. It includes an embedded offline 3D renderer shim and synthesized Web Audio sound effects so it works 100% offline on Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, and Linux.

            

            

              Does FLUFFY require any API keys, paid servers, or external 3D models?
              
No. Every 3D mesh, hair filament, shader, animation state, and sound effect is procedurally generated in clean JavaScript and GLSL with zero external asset files or paid APIs.
