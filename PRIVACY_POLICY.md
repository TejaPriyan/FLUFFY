# Privacy Policy for FLUFFY — Your Little World of Fluff

**Last Updated:** October 2026

1. **100% Client-Side Execution:** FLUFFY executes entirely within your web browser using WebGL2 and Web Audio API.
2. **Zero Data Collection:** We do not collect, transmit, log, or share any personal information, telemetry, or cookies.
3. **Local Storage Only:** Customization preferences (pet name, coat color, accessory, and stats) are saved strictly on your own device via `window.localStorage` and can be cleared anytime via the in-app *Reset Saved Settings* button.
4. **Offline Usage:** The downloaded offline application (`fluffy_offline_app.html`) functions completely without internet access.
